## Reading the matrix

* G1, G2, G3, G4 and G5 are gaps in the sense of category C of the review: I found no earlier work
  that closes them. They are gaps about *probabilistic quality and principled combination*, not
  about raw localisation accuracy, where stronger methods exist (SmartFL, learned combinations).
* G6, G7, G8 and G9 are deficiencies of the project that must be fixed for any claim to stand.
* G10 and G11 are real weaknesses that the selected work does not address.
